# Business Insights Report

## Executive Summary
The analysis focuses on revenue, profitability, customer behavior, product performance and regional sales.

## Analysis Areas
1. **Revenue trend:** Monthly sales are reviewed to identify seasonality and growth/decline periods.
2. **Category performance:** Categories are ranked by revenue, units and profit margin.
3. **Product performance:** Top products are identified using revenue and profit.
4. **Customer value:** Customers are ranked using lifetime revenue and profit.
5. **Regional performance:** Regions are compared on sales, profit and order volume.
6. **Retention:** Repeat customer rate is calculated to understand customer loyalty.
7. **Operations:** Return and cancellation rates highlight potential service/product issues.

## Recommended Business Actions
- Increase inventory and marketing support for consistently high-margin categories.
- Investigate products with high sales but weak profit margins.
- Create retention campaigns for one-time customers.
- Use high-value customer segments for loyalty programs.
- Review regions with strong order volume but weaker profitability.
- Track returns and cancellations by product/category to identify root causes.

> Note: The dataset is synthetic and intended for portfolio/learning purposes.
